# Pantry Club loyalty program

_Tom's Kitchen customer policy handbook · Section 6 · Last updated 2026-09-01_

Pantry Club is free to join. Create an account and you're a member.

## 6.1 Earning points

- Members earn **1 point per $1** of merchandise paid for, **after** promo-code and verified discounts.
- **Silver** members earn **1.25 points per $1** and **Gold** members earn **1.5 points per $1**.
- No points are earned on shipping, tax, heavy-item surcharges, remote-delivery fees, or on gift card purchases.
- Points are added when the order ships and are rounded down to a whole point on each order.
- **Birthday bonus:** 200 points in your birthday month, if your birthday is saved in your account at least 30 days beforehand.
- **Reviews:** 25 points for a product review with a photo, up to 10 reviews a year.

## 6.2 Using points

- Every **100 points** turns automatically into a **$5 reward**, sent by email and saved in your account.
- Rewards are used as a form of payment at checkout. They can be combined with promo codes and verified discounts.
- Rewards expire **90 days** after they're issued.
- Unconverted points expire if your account has **no purchase for 12 months**.

## 6.3 Tiers

Your tier is based on the **points earned in a calendar year** (January 1 to December 31). Birthday and review points count toward your tier.

| Tier | Points earned in the calendar year | Benefits |
|---|---|---|
| **Member** | 0–499 | 1 point per $1; one free knife sharpening a year (3.5); early access to Kitchen Flash emails |
| **Silver** | 500–1,499 | 1.25 points per $1; **free standard shipping on every order**, no minimum |
| **Gold** | 1,500 or more | 1.5 points per $1; free standard shipping on every order; **free return labels**, including heavy items (1.7); **free upgrade to expedited shipping** on orders with a merchandise subtotal of $75 or more (contiguous US only); invitations to product previews |

- You move up as soon as you pass the threshold, and the new earning rate applies from your next order.
- Once you reach a tier, you **keep it for the rest of that calendar year and all of the next one**.
- Each tier includes every benefit of the tiers below it.

## 6.4 Returns and points

If you return an item, the points earned on it are removed. If that takes your balance below zero, the difference is taken from future points; we never charge you for it. Returns can also lower your tier progress for the current year, but they never remove a tier you have already reached.

## 6.5 Program changes

We may change the program with 30 days' notice by email. Points and rewards already earned will be honored under the rules in place when they were earned.
