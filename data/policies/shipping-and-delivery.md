# Shipping and delivery

_Tom's Kitchen customer policy handbook · Section 2 · Last updated 2026-09-01_

Everything ships from our warehouse in Columbus, Ohio. This page covers where we ship, what it costs, how fast it gets there, and what to do if a package is late, lost or damaged.

## 2.1 Where we ship

We ship to all 50 US states, Washington DC, Puerto Rico, and APO/FPO/DPO military addresses. We do not currently ship to Canada or any other country, and we cannot ship to freight forwarders.

PO boxes are fine for most orders. Items that carry a heavy-item surcharge cannot be shipped to a PO box; they need a street address.

## 2.2 Shipping options and prices

| Service | Transit time after it ships | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 3–6 business days | **$6.95** per order, or **free** when the merchandise subtotal is **$75 or more** |
| Expedited | 2 business days | **$14.95** per order, never free (but see Pantry Club Gold in Section 6) |

How the free-shipping threshold is worked out:

- The **merchandise subtotal** is the total of the items in the order **after** promo-code discounts and **before** tax.
- Gift cards, heavy-item surcharges and Pantry Club rewards redeemed as payment do not count toward the $75. Gift cards are shipped by email and never incur shipping.
- Pantry Club Silver and Gold members get free standard shipping on every order, with no minimum.

If you return items and your remaining order falls below $75, we don't charge shipping after the fact.

## 2.3 Heavy-item surcharges

A few large or heavy items (such as enameled cast iron Dutch ovens and large stockpots) cost much more to ship. Each of these has a **heavy-item surcharge** listed on its product page and in the catalog. The surcharge:

- is charged **per unit** (two Dutch ovens means two surcharges),
- is charged **even when the order ships free**,
- is not reduced by promo codes or the Pantry Club,
- is not refunded on change-of-mind returns (see 1.7), and
- is refunded in full if the item is faulty, arrived damaged, or we sent the wrong item.

Heavy items can ship by standard or expedited service to the contiguous US. They cannot ship to PO boxes or APO/FPO addresses.

## 2.4 Alaska, Hawaii, Puerto Rico and military addresses

- Orders to **Alaska, Hawaii and Puerto Rico** add a flat **$20 per order**, in addition to any standard shipping charge and heavy-item surcharges. This remote-delivery fee applies even when standard shipping is free.
- **Expedited shipping is not available** to Alaska, Hawaii, Puerto Rico or APO/FPO/DPO addresses.
- Standard transit to these areas is 7–12 business days.
- APO/FPO/DPO orders ship by standard service only, with no remote-delivery fee, and can take 2–4 weeks.

## 2.5 When your order ships

- Orders placed by **1:00 pm Eastern time, Monday to Friday**, ship the **same business day**.
- Orders placed after 1:00 pm, or on a weekend or US federal holiday, ship the **next business day**.
- Items flagged **ships separately** in the catalog (mainly large appliances and big cookware sets) leave in their own box and may arrive on a different day, with their own tracking number. You pay shipping only once per order.
- Pre-order and back-ordered items ship when they arrive in stock. We charge your card when the item ships, not when you order it.

## 2.6 Changing or cancelling an order

You can change or cancel an order until it moves to **Packing** status. That usually happens within **one hour** of ordering during warehouse hours (8:00 am–6:00 pm ET, Monday to Friday). Orders placed overnight or at the weekend stay editable until the warehouse opens.

Once an order is Packing, it can no longer be changed. You can still return it after delivery under Section 1. If you meant to order a different color, an exchange (1.6) is free.

## 2.7 Tracking

We email a tracking link when your order ships. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show the first scan. We use UPS, FedEx and USPS and choose the carrier for each package; we can't ship with a specific carrier on request.

## 2.8 Late and lost packages

- If tracking has shown **no movement for 7 calendar days** (5 for expedited), contact us. We open a trace with the carrier and, at the same time, **reship your order or refund it**, whichever you prefer. You don't have to wait for the trace to finish.
- If tracking says **delivered** but you don't have the package, check with neighbors and around the property, and wait one more day (carriers sometimes scan early). Then contact us within **14 days** of the delivery scan and we will investigate and reship.
- If you paid for expedited shipping and the order arrives late through the carrier's fault, we refund the expedited fee. Weather delays are excluded.

## 2.9 Damaged in transit

If an item arrives damaged, report it within **7 days of delivery** with a photo of the item and the box. We send a free replacement right away, or a full refund if you prefer, including shipping and any heavy-item surcharge. For broken glass or ceramics, don't send the pieces back; we'll ask you to dispose of them safely.

## 2.10 Signature and delivery instructions

- Orders with a merchandise subtotal over **$500** require an adult signature at delivery.
- You can add delivery instructions (for example "leave at side door") at checkout. Carriers try to follow them but don't guarantee it.
- We can't change a delivery address once an order has shipped. The carrier may let you redirect the package through their own website.
