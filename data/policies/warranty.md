# Product warranty

_Tom's Kitchen customer policy handbook · Section 3 · Last updated 2026-09-01_

Every Tom's Kitchen product carries a limited warranty against manufacturing defects. The length depends on the product and is listed as **warranty months** on each product page and in the catalog. This page explains what the warranty covers, what it doesn't, and how to make a claim.

## 3.1 How long the warranty lasts

- The warranty starts on the **delivery date** of your order and lasts for the number of months shown for that product.
- Typical lengths: 12 months for most small appliances, storage, prep tools and coffee accessories; 24 months for nonstick and carbon steel cookware and for premium appliances such as kettles, grinders and espresso machines; 120 months (10 years) for tri-ply stainless, enameled cast iron and forged knives. Always check the product's own listing; the catalog is the final word.
- The warranty covers the original purchaser and anyone who received the item as a gift, as long as they can give us the order number.
- Items bought from other retailers are covered too, with proof of purchase showing the date.
- A **replacement** sent under warranty is covered for the rest of the original warranty or for 90 days, whichever is longer.
- Final sale, clearance and open-box items carry the same warranty as full-price items.

## 3.2 What the warranty covers

Defects in materials or workmanship that show up under normal household use, for example:

- An appliance that stops working, won't heat, won't hold temperature, or trips the power
- A handle that loosens and can't be tightened, or rivets that fail
- Nonstick coating that **peels, flakes or blisters** under normal use (not scratches; see below)
- Enamel on cast iron that **chips or crazes** without an impact
- Warping of a pan's base under normal heat
- Knife blades that crack, or handles that split or separate from the tang
- Lids, seals and gaskets that fail to seal when new
- Glass that cracks under the normal temperature range listed for that product

## 3.3 What the warranty does not cover

- **Normal wear and tear**, including scratches and gradual loss of release on nonstick coatings, dulling of knife edges, and wear on cutting boards
- **Cosmetic changes that don't affect performance**: discoloration or "rainbow" tinting on stainless steel, patina and color changes on carbon steel, seasoning wear on cast iron, staining of enamel
- **Misuse**: metal utensils or abrasive pads on nonstick; putting items that are not dishwasher safe in the dishwasher; heating an empty nonstick pan on high; thermal shock (cold water on a hot pan, cast iron or glass); dropping; using knives on bone, frozen food, glass or stone; using a mandoline without its guard
- **Electrical misuse**: every Tom's Kitchen appliance is built for **120 V, 60 Hz** North American power only. Using an appliance with a **voltage converter or transformer**, or outside the US, Canada and other 120 V countries, **voids its warranty**. Plug adapters that only change the plug shape do not change the voltage and will damage the appliance.
- **Commercial use** in restaurants, cafés, catering or other businesses
- Repairs or modifications by anyone other than Tom's Kitchen or a service center we authorize
- Consumable parts: water filters, blender gaskets after the first 12 months, coffee grinder burrs after the first 12 months, and batteries

## 3.4 Nonstick coatings

Our nonstick coatings are PFOA-free. Nonstick coating has its own limit: whatever the warranty on the pan's body, the **coating itself is covered for 24 months** against peeling, flaking and blistering. After 24 months, coating wear is treated as normal wear and tear.

## 3.5 Knives and sharpening

Forged knives carry a 10-year warranty against defects. Chips from hard use, bent tips and dishwasher damage aren't defects.

We also offer a **mail-in sharpening service**. Pantry Club members get **one free sharpening per calendar year** (up to four knives in one shipment, with a prepaid label). Otherwise it costs **$12 per knife**, including return shipping. Serrated knives are sharpened for free for everyone, because they rarely need it. Sharpening is not available for ceramic blades.

## 3.6 How to make a warranty claim

1. Email **support@tomskitchen.example** or use the chat on our website. Include your **order number** (or proof of purchase), the **product**, a short description of the problem, and a **photo or short video** that shows it.
2. We reply within **2 business days**.
3. If the claim is approved, we fix it in this order of preference:
   1. **Send a replacement part** (lids, handles, gaskets, baskets, beaters and similar) if that fully fixes the problem.
   2. **Replace the product** with the same item, or the closest current equivalent if it's no longer sold.
   3. If no equivalent exists, issue **store credit or a refund for the price originally paid**, whichever you prefer.
4. We send a free prepaid label if we need the item back. Often we don't, and we'll tell you how to dispose of it.
5. During the first 30 days after delivery, you can choose a **full refund** instead of a replacement for any approved warranty claim.

## 3.7 Known issues

When we find a problem that affects a batch of products, we publish it here and fix it proactively.

- **KI-001 · Rapid-Boil Electric Kettle 1.7L (KT-1001):** on units shipped before 2026-09-01, the lid latch may not fully engage, so the lid can pop open while pouring. Remedy: a **free replacement lid assembly**, or a **full replacement kettle** on request. This remedy applies regardless of purchase date or warranty status, and no photo is needed. Customers who report the problem should be told to stop using the kettle until the new lid arrives.

## 3.8 Safety concerns

If a product has caused an injury, burned, melted, sparked or smoked, stop using it, unplug it if it is electric, and contact us right away. These reports go straight to our product safety team and are handled the same business day. Please keep the product, because we may need it to investigate.
