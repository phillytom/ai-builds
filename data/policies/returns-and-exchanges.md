# Returns and exchanges

_Tom's Kitchen customer policy handbook · Section 1 · Last updated 2026-09-01_

We want everything you buy from Tom's Kitchen to earn its place in your kitchen. If it doesn't, this page explains how to send it back, what you get back, and how long it takes. The short version: unused items can come back within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, and most cookware and knives can come back even after you've cooked with them, for store credit, within 60 days.

All windows on this page are counted in calendar days from the **delivery date** shown in your order's tracking, not the order date. If tracking has no delivery scan, we use the carrier's estimated delivery date plus two days.

## 1.1 Standard returns (30 days, unused)

Any item that is unused and complete can be returned within **30 days of delivery** for a full refund of the price you paid for the item.

- "Unused" means the item has not been cooked with, plugged in, filled with food or liquid, washed, or sharpened. Unboxing, handling and looking are fine.
- "Complete" means every part, accessory, manual and insert that came in the box is included. The original box is preferred, but we accept unused items in any sturdy box as long as the item is protected.
- The refund covers the item price actually paid, after any promo-code discount allocated to that item. Sales tax on the item is refunded too.
- Original shipping charges are not refunded, except as described in 1.7.

## 1.2 The Cook With It Guarantee (60 days, used, store credit)

You can't know whether a pan suits you until you've cooked with it. So for two categories we accept returns of **used** items:

- **Cookware** (all skillets, saucepans, sauté pans, stockpots, Dutch ovens, woks, grill pans, bakeware and roasting pans)
- **Knives & prep** (knives, cutting boards, mandolines, peelers, graters and similar tools), **except** personalized or engraved items (see 1.4)

Terms:

1. The return must reach us within **60 days of delivery**.
2. You receive **Tom's Kitchen store credit** for the item price paid. The Cook With It Guarantee never refunds to a card or PayPal. (If an item is also within the 30-day window and unused, section 1.1 applies instead and you can choose a refund to the original payment method.)
3. It covers normal use. It does not cover damage from misuse: metal utensils or abrasive pads on nonstick coatings, putting items that are not dishwasher safe in the dishwasher, overheating an empty pan, dropping, or using a knife on bone, frozen food, glass or stone.
4. It can be used **once per SKU per customer**. If you return a 10" skillet under the guarantee, a later 10" skillet of the same SKU is covered by the standard policy only.
5. Small appliances, storage and coffee gear are **not** covered by the Cook With It Guarantee. Used items in those categories can only come back if they are faulty, under the warranty (Section 3).

Carbon steel and cast iron items are expected to change color as they build seasoning. That is normal use, and it doesn't reduce your store credit.

## 1.3 Small appliances

Small appliances (kettles, toasters, blenders, air fryers, mixers, rice cookers, and the electric coffee gear such as grinders and espresso machines) can be returned within 30 days only if they are unused and complete, under 1.1. Once an appliance has been plugged in and used, we can only take it back if it is faulty. In that case the warranty applies (Section 3), and within the first 30 days after delivery we will give you a full refund instead of a replacement if you prefer.

## 1.4 Final sale items

Some items cannot be returned or exchanged for any reason other than a defect:

- Items marked **final sale** in the catalog (these are usually clearance items, and the product page shows a "Final sale" badge)
- **Personalized or engraved items**, including engraved knives and monogrammed cutting boards
- **Gift cards**
- **Open-box items** sold through our Second Helpings outlet

Final sale items are still covered by their product warranty. If a final sale item arrives faulty or damaged, or it develops a manufacturing defect during the warranty period, contact us as described in Section 3.

## 1.5 Holiday returns

Items **delivered between November 1 and December 24** can be returned under the standard 30-day policy (1.1) until **January 31** of the following year, if that date is later than the normal 30-day deadline. Every other rule in 1.1 still applies: the item must be unused and complete.

The holiday extension applies to standard returns only. It does not extend the 60-day Cook With It Guarantee window, which is always 60 days from delivery. It does not make final sale items returnable.

## 1.6 Exchanges

Want a different color or size of the same product? Unused items can be exchanged within 30 days of delivery at no charge. We send the replacement as soon as the carrier scans your return, so you are not left waiting. If the new item costs more, we charge the difference; if it costs less, we refund the difference to your original payment method. Exchanges for a different product are handled as a return plus a new order.

## 1.7 Return shipping

We email you a prepaid return label when you start a return in your account or with our support team.

| Situation | Return label | Original shipping and surcharges |
|---|---|---|
| Item is faulty, arrived damaged, or we sent the wrong item | Free | Refunded in full, including any heavy-item surcharge |
| Changed your mind (standard return or Cook With It Guarantee) | **$7.95**, deducted from the refund or credit | Not refunded |
| Changed your mind, item carries a heavy-item surcharge | **$19.95**, deducted from the refund or credit | Not refunded, and the heavy-item surcharge is not refunded |
| Changed your mind, Pantry Club **Gold** member | Free (standard and heavy items) | Not refunded |
| Exchange for a different color or size (1.6) | Free | n/a |

You are welcome to use your own carrier instead of our label. You then pay that carrier directly, no label fee is deducted, and you should use a tracked service, because we cannot refund a return that never arrives.

If several items from one order go back in one box on one label, the label fee is charged once. If they need separate boxes, each box is a separate label.

## 1.8 Returning part of a bundle

Bundles (for example the Everyday Cookware Bundle) are sold at a discount compared with buying the pieces separately. You can return a whole bundle, or just some of its pieces.

- **Whole bundle:** refund of the bundle price paid.
- **Part of a bundle:** the bundle discount is shared out across the pieces in proportion to their individual list prices. The refund for a returned piece is:

  > piece list price × (bundle price paid ÷ sum of the list prices of all pieces in the bundle), rounded to the nearest cent

  For example, a bundle that sells for $90 made of three pieces listed at $40, $40 and $20 (total $100) refunds $36.00 for either $40 piece and $18.00 for the $20 piece.

Every other rule in this section (windows, label fees, the Cook With It Guarantee) applies to each returned piece as if it had been bought on its own.

## 1.9 Where your refund goes

- Refunds go back to the **original payment method**.
- If you paid with a mix of a gift card and another method, the refund is split in the same proportions as the original payment. The gift card share comes back as a new e-gift card, sent to the email on the order. The other share goes back to the card or PayPal account.
- Any deduction (such as a return label fee) is taken from the card or PayPal share first, and only from the gift card share if the card share is not enough.
- Store credit from the Cook With It Guarantee is added to your Tom's Kitchen account and never expires.
- Pantry Club points earned on a returned item are removed from your balance (Section 6).

## 1.10 How long it takes

- Our warehouse in Columbus, Ohio inspects returns within **3 business days** of receiving them.
- We email you when the refund or credit is issued.
- Refunds to cards take **5–10 business days** to appear, depending on your bank. PayPal refunds usually appear within 2 business days. Store credit and e-gift cards are available immediately.

## 1.11 Gifts

If you received an item as a gift, you can return it with the gift receipt or the order number. Gift returns are refunded as Tom's Kitchen store credit to the gift recipient, so the person who sent the gift is not notified. The windows above count from the delivery date of the original order.

## 1.12 Refused and undeliverable packages

If you refuse a delivery, or a package comes back to us because the address could not be reached, we refund the item price once the package is back in our warehouse. Original shipping charges and heavy-item surcharges are not refunded, and a $7.95 return fee ($19.95 for heavy items) is deducted to cover the carrier's return charge.

## 1.13 What we can't accept

- Items bought from other retailers, even if they are Tom's Kitchen brand. Please return them to the store you bought them from.
- Items that are more than 60 days past delivery (or past January 31 for holiday returns), unless they are faulty and within warranty.
- Items sent back without a return authorization. We will try to match them to an order, but this can take several weeks.
