# Frequently asked questions

_Tom's Kitchen customer policy handbook · Section 8 · Last updated 2026-09-01_

Short answers to the questions our support team hears most. Where a question has a fuller answer elsewhere in the handbook, the section number is in brackets.

## Contacting us

**How do I reach support?**
Email support@tomskitchen.example, use the chat on our website, or call 1-800-555-0142. Chat and phone are staffed **8:00 am–8:00 pm ET Monday to Friday** and **10:00 am–4:00 pm ET Saturday**. We answer email within one business day, and billing and safety questions first.

**Do you have a store I can visit?**
No. Tom's Kitchen sells online only. Our Columbus, Ohio warehouse isn't open to the public, and local pickup isn't available.

## Orders

**Can I change the color on an order I just placed?**
Yes, if it hasn't reached Packing status, which usually happens within an hour during warehouse hours. After that, order the right color and return the other one, or take a free exchange after delivery. [2.6, 1.6]

**I entered the wrong order number when I emailed. Is that a problem?**
No. We can find orders by email address, name or delivery address. Tom's Kitchen order numbers look like **TK-10201**, and tracking numbers start with **TK** followed by the carrier code.

**Can I order something that's out of stock?**
Out-of-stock products show an expected restock date. For most, you can place a back-order; we charge you when it ships. You can also tap "Email me when it's back." Restock dates are estimates and can move.

**Do you offer gift wrap?**
Not at the moment. Every order ships with a packing slip that shows no prices, and you can add a gift message at checkout. A gift receipt link is emailed to you, to forward to the recipient if you like.

**Can I order for delivery outside the US?**
Not yet. We ship to all 50 states, DC, Puerto Rico and military addresses only. [2.1]

## Products

**Will this pan work on my induction hob?**
Check the **induction compatible** field in the product's listing. As a rule: tri-ply stainless, enameled cast iron and carbon steel work on induction; our standard aluminium nonstick pans don't, unless the listing says "induction". [7.1, 7.2]

**Can I put it in the dishwasher?**
Check the **dishwasher safe** field in the product's listing. Knives, wooden items, cast iron and carbon steel are never dishwasher safe. [Section 7]

**Are your nonstick pans PFOA-free?**
Yes, all of them.

**Can I use my appliance abroad?**
Our appliances are made for 120 V, 60 Hz North American power only. Using them with a voltage converter voids the warranty, and a simple plug adapter will damage them. If you're moving to a 220–240 V country, we'd suggest buying appliances locally. Cookware, knives and storage work anywhere. [3.3]

**Where are your products made?**
The country of origin is listed for each product in the catalog. We design everything in-house and work with factories in the US, Portugal, France, Japan, Germany, Italy, China, India and Vietnam.

**Do you sell replacement parts?**
Yes, lids, baskets, gaskets, beaters and cups for most current products. Ask support for the part you need. Parts for faulty items are free under warranty. [3.6]

**Can you engrave a knife?**
Yes, on forged knives, up to 20 characters, for $15 per knife. Engraving adds 3 business days before the order ships. Engraved knives are final sale. [1.4]

## Returns, refunds and money

**How do I start a return?**
In your account, open the order and choose "Start a return", or ask support. We'll email a prepaid label. [1.7]

**How much will I get back?**
The item price you paid, less any return label fee for change-of-mind returns. Original shipping and heavy-item surcharges aren't refunded unless the problem was ours. [1.1, 1.7]

**When will I see my refund?**
We process returns within 3 business days of receiving them. Card refunds then take 5–10 business days to appear. [1.10]

**I see two charges for one order.**
That's usually an authorization plus the final charge, and the authorization drops off within 3–5 business days. If it's still there after 5 business days, contact us. [5.3]

**The price dropped right after I bought it. Can I get the difference?**
Yes, if it dropped within 14 days of delivery, on our own site, and the item wasn't final sale or a Kitchen Flash price. [4.2]

**Do you price match?**
Only our own prices (see above). We don't match other retailers. [4.2]

## Pantry Club

Create an account. Membership is free and automatic. [Section 6]

**How do I get Gold?**
Earn 1,500 points in a calendar year. Gold lasts for the rest of that year and the whole of the next. [6.3]

## Safety

**My appliance sparked, smoked or melted.**
Stop using it and unplug it straight away, then contact us. Safety reports are handled the same business day. [3.8]

**The lid on my kettle pops open.**
If it's the Rapid-Boil Electric Kettle (KT-1001), that's a known issue on some units. Stop using it and contact us for a free replacement lid assembly or a new kettle. [3.7]
