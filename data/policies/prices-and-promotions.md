# Prices, price adjustments and promotions

_Tom's Kitchen customer policy handbook · Section 4 · Last updated 2026-09-01_

## 4.1 Our prices

Prices on tomskitchen.example are in US dollars and do not include sales tax. The catalog shows two prices for each product:

- **List price**: the regular everyday price.
- **Sale price**: shown when the item is on sale. If there's no sale price, the list price applies.

We sometimes run **Kitchen Flash** events: sales on a small group of products that last 24 hours or less, announced by email and on the home page.

## 4.2 Price adjustments

If the price of an item you bought from us **drops within 14 days of delivery**, we refund the difference.

- It must be the **same SKU** (any color or finish of that SKU counts).
- The lower price must be our own price on tomskitchen.example. **We do not match other retailers' prices.**
- Price adjustments are **not available** for:
  - items marked **final sale** or clearance, whether you bought them at full price or not,
  - **Kitchen Flash** prices,
  - **bundle** prices (a bundle getting cheaper, or a piece of a bundle being sold for less on its own),
  - prices that need a promo code, or member-only or verified-discount prices,
  - prices during the Black Friday to Cyber Monday weekend.
- Each item can get **one price adjustment**. If the price drops twice, we adjust to the lowest price available on the day you ask.
- The difference goes back to your original payment method. Ask through chat or email with your order number. We don't adjust prices automatically.
- If you'd rather return the item and buy it again at the lower price, that's a normal return under Section 1 (label fees apply).

## 4.3 Promo codes

- **One promo code per order.** Codes can't be combined with each other or with verified discounts (4.5).
- Promo codes **don't apply** to gift cards, bundles, final sale or clearance items, Kitchen Flash prices, heavy-item surcharges, shipping or tax.
- If an order has items the code doesn't cover, the code discounts only the items it does cover.
- The discount is shared across the eligible items in proportion to their price. That share is what's refunded if you return an item.
- Codes can't be added after an order is placed. If you forgot one, contact us within 7 days and we'll apply it as a refund, as long as the code was valid on the day you ordered.

## 4.4 WELCOME10

New customers can use **WELCOME10** for **10% off their first order**, with a **maximum discount of $50**. It works once per customer and household, and not on an order placed as a guest after an account was already used at the same address. All the promo code rules in 4.3 apply.

## 4.5 Verified discounts

Students, teachers, nurses, first responders and military members (active duty and veterans) get **15% off** full-price items after a one-time verification through our partner at checkout. Verified discounts can't be combined with promo codes or used on sale, clearance, bundle or Kitchen Flash prices. They can be combined with Pantry Club rewards.

## 4.6 Pantry Club rewards

Pantry Club rewards (Section 6) are treated as a **form of payment**, not a discount. They can be used together with a promo code or a verified discount, and on sale items.

## 4.7 Pricing errors

If an item is listed at an obviously wrong price because of a mistake, we may cancel the order and refund you in full, even after sending an order confirmation. If the order has already shipped, you can keep the item at the price you paid or return it at our expense.

## 4.8 Sales tax

Sales tax is worked out from the delivery address and the tax rules of that state. Tax is charged on shipping in states that require it. Orders to Delaware, Montana, New Hampshire and Oregon don't pay state sales tax. Tax-exempt organizations can apply through support before ordering; we can't refund tax on orders that were already placed.
