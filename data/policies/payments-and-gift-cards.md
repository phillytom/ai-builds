# Payments and gift cards

_Tom's Kitchen customer policy handbook · Section 5 · Last updated 2026-09-01_

## 5.1 Ways to pay

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Tom's Kitchen gift cards, store credit and Pantry Club rewards. You can combine gift cards, store credit and rewards with one card or PayPal account on a single order. We don't accept checks, cash on delivery or cryptocurrency.

## 5.2 Pay in 4

Orders with a total between **$50 and $1,000** can be split into **four interest-free payments** every two weeks, through our payment partner at checkout. The first payment is taken when the order ships. Pay in 4 can't be combined with gift cards or store credit on the same order. Refunds on Pay in 4 orders go back to the payment plan, reducing the remaining payments first.

## 5.3 When we charge you

We **authorize** your card when you place the order and **charge** it when the order ships. For back-ordered items, we charge when each item ships.

Some banks show the authorization and the final charge as **two separate transactions** for a few days. This is not a double charge. The authorization is released automatically, usually within **3–5 business days**, depending on your bank. If a duplicate still appears after 5 business days, contact us with the order number and the last four digits of the card, and we'll send your bank a confirmation to release it.

If you believe you've really been charged twice, please contact us before disputing with your bank. We reply to billing questions within one business day, and a dispute can freeze your order while the bank investigates.

## 5.4 Gift cards

- Tom's Kitchen **e-gift cards** come in any amount from **$25 to $500** and are delivered by email, either right away or on a date you choose.
- Gift cards **never expire**, and there are no fees.
- Gift cards **can't be returned or refunded**, and can't be redeemed for cash, except where state law requires it.
- Gift cards can't be used to buy other gift cards.
- You can use up to **five gift cards** on a single order.
- Lost a gift card email? We can resend it or replace the card if you give us the order number it was bought on, or the recipient's email address. We can't replace a gift card that has already been spent.
- Check a balance any time on our Gift Cards page.

## 5.5 Store credit

Store credit comes from returns under the Cook With It Guarantee, gift returns, and some goodwill gestures from our support team. It is stored in your Tom's Kitchen account, **never expires**, can be used on anything including gift cards, and can't be transferred to another account or exchanged for cash.

## 5.6 Payment security

We never store full card numbers; payments are handled by our PCI-compliant payment provider. Our support team will **never** ask for your full card number, security code or password by email, chat or phone. If you receive a message like that, it isn't from us.
